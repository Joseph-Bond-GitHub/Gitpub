How this works

The terminal on the right is a simulated bash talking to a JHipster 8 app at http://localhost:8080. It runs entirely in this page and doesn't touch your real app. There are two apps, which you choose in the toolbar. dbwp is your week 7 chatbot app, built from the lab-solution JDL. bookshop is a JDL you haven't seen, like the one the test will give you. Both follow JHipster's conventions: JWT login, sort/page/size, X-Total-Count and Link, 400 from validation versus 500 from database constraints, the id rules on PUT and PATCH, 204 DELETE, and Problem-JSON errors. jq, grep -o, cut and shell variables all work, so the lab's TOKEN=$(… | grep -o … | cut …) runs as written.

For a LAB question, write the curl command, run it, then type the result into the box. The terminal switches to that question's app when you click into its answer box. Answers are checked against the simulator's current data, so press Reset DB when a question says to. The grey ← 200 OK line under each response is a study aid that real curl doesn't print. Use -i or -w '%{http_code}' to get the status the real way.

Where I'm not certain JHipster behaves exactly as simulated, the explanation says so, and the Check on your app tab lists commands to confirm it on your lab app. Your lab sheet and its solutions overrule everything here.
